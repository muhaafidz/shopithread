💡 Shopee Affiliate & CSV Dashboard Extension Guide

Easy steps to collect Shopee products and export them to CSV.

1

Open the Shopee Affiliate Portal

Log in to your affiliate account at affiliate.shopee.com.my/offer/product_offer.

2

Click the "📦 Affiliate Tools" Button

At the bottom right of the Shopee page, click the orange floating button to open the control panel.

3

Choose Target Pages & Save

Select how many pages to scrape (1 to 10 pages), then click "💾 Save to Dashboard" or "⚡ Generate ZIP".

4

Manage & Export in the Dashboard

All saved products will automatically appear in this table. You can copy affiliate links, edit data, or download CSV files anytime.
